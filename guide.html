STEP 02

Before you report

The official reporting portal may ask for information about what happened and supporting information where applicable.

The information requested can vary depending on the type of complaint.

JustReport does not collect your personal information or evidence.

STEP 03

How to report

If you decide to report the incident, you can use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

01

Open the official portal

Open the Government of India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Open official portal ↗
02

Choose the relevant route

The official portal provides different routes for reporting cybercrime.

Women / Children Related Crime For complaints covered by this section of the official portal.
Financial Fraud For online financial fraud.
Other Cyber Crime For other cybercrime complaints covered by the portal.
03

Follow the instructions

The information and registration process can vary depending on the reporting route.

Follow the instructions displayed by the official portal.

04

Provide the requested information

Describe what happened clearly and provide the information requested by the official portal.

JustReport does not collect this information.

05

Review and submit

Review the information you have entered and submit your complaint directly through the official portal.

IMPORTANT

JustReport is only an informational guide. It does not receive, store, or submit complaints.

Any information you provide for your complaint goes directly to the official reporting service.
